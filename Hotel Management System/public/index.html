Why Dual Databases? (MySQL + MongoDB)
MySQL manages the core relational model with ACID transactions, foreign keys, and strict relational schemas (Hotels, Rooms, Bookings, Payments).
MongoDB handles unstructured, append-heavy audit trails (activity_logs) and guest reviews (feedback) with dynamic document structures and rapid timeline queries.
