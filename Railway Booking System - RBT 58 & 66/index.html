Essential Indian Rail Travel Rules

Key guidelines and safety advisories for a smooth and hassle-free railway journey.

Tatkal Booking Hours

Tatkal reservations open at 10:00 AM for AC classes (1A, 2A, 3A, CC) and at 11:00 AM for Non-AC classes (Sleeper, 2S) one day in advance of travel date.

Valid ID Proof Mandate

At least one passenger on an e-ticket must carry an original government ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter Card, Driving License, Passport or Pan Card) during the journey for TTE verification.

Free Cancellation Protection

Opt for Free Cancellation during booking to claim 100% refund without deduction of IRCTC clerkage fees if cancelled before chart preparation (usually 4 hours prior).
