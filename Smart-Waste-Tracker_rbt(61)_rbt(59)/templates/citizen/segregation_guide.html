Why Segregation Matters

Proper waste segregation at source reduces the burden on landfills, protects sanitation personnel from hazardous materials, and enables efficient recovery of recyclable paper, plastics, and metals. Composted wet organic waste produces fertile soil manure for urban agriculture.
